---
title: "I'm sorry"
subtitle: "When I look at the future, there isn't much hope"
---

Life was always expected to get better with each generation and throughout history, it did always work that way. 
But I don't see it anymore. 

It's been easy to notice the people coming after me are living harder lives than those that came before. As a society, we've stopped planting trees and started burning them. The generation before me no longer wants to improve this country, but only seeks a comfortable death. 
Those that were raised during the boom years, have squandered the world. Their goal is to drain us and die. Without worry. Without care. Having pushed their next generation into fire to keep themselves warm, instead of forward, closer to prosperity. 

And while we grew up with and entered adulthood with dwindling opportunities, you may grow up with less. 

There will be fierce competition for what's left. There will be despondency that hard work will leave you not better off than your parents. 

Because we have designed this economy in a way that if you aren't born rich, it's very unlikely that you will ever become wealthy. And with the cutthroat work culture developing, we are shoving down anyone still trying to climb the ladder. 

AI is not creating wealth. Right now, it is cementing the classes. I'm worried for you buddy.
