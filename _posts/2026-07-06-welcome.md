title: "First"
subtitle: "I don't know what this will be"
---
I'll be telling you, you have to start somewhere. Even if you're uncomfortable, the most important thing is to keep putting one foot in front of the other and keep moving in a direction. 
Is the direction correct?
Are you moving fast enough?
What will others think?

There are lots of worries, but the biggest worry should be of stagnation. Stagnant water turns rotten after all. Stagnant air fosters an environment where heavy pollution hangs. Our biggest constant should be change, no matter the trouble it brings. The most regretful times of my life have been without a lot of change. Rest is good, but rest is also transforming if it's good rest.

I don't know how old you'll be when you read this, or what you'll already know about me by then. So I'll just start.

Right now, today, you aren't here yet. You're coming. The Mayo clinic book said you're about the size of a peach right now. Smaller than a fist. Isn't that wierd. I saw a rendition of what you might look like. It feels strange to know you're growing from that little thing. 

I think of things that I want you to know, habits that I'd like you to have, lessons I want you to learn, values that I'd like you to discover, and I wonder how these can come up organically without being forced. I don't know how to do it - but hopefully I'll think of it before it's too late. Maybe I'll write some of it down here. 

Life is changing so fast buddy. The more I pay attention to what's happening in the world, the more I find that I lose sight of myself and my immediate surroundings. I don't think others have found a balance either. I wonder if everyone needs to make a choice on what matters to them. I don't know if I have yet. 

I hope you'll be good.
