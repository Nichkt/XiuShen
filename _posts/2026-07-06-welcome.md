title: "The first one"
subtitle: "Written before I really knew what this would become"
---
I don't know how old you'll be when you read this, or what you'll already know about me by then. So I'll just start.

Right now, today, you are still small enough that most of what I want to teach you hasn't come up yet. I keep thinking about the things I want you to have — not things I can hand you, but things I want you to *know*, in the way you only know something after it's been said to you plainly, more than once, by someone who meant it.

So that's what this is. Not a diary of what happened. More like a running note of what I was thinking, and what I hoped for you, written close to when I actually felt it — before I had the chance to clean it up or make it sound wiser than it was.

I'll add to this when something feels worth keeping. You can read it whenever you want. All of it, or none of it, or just the parts that matter to you at the time.
