<h1 class="post-title" style="margin-bottom: 24px;">About this place</h1>

<div class="post-content">
<p>If you're reading this, you found it. Good.</p>

<p>This is where I've been writing down things I want you to know — not all at once, and not as a lecture. Just notes, written in the moment, about whatever I was thinking or learning or worried about or proud of on that day. Some of it will be about you directly. Some of it will just be me trying to work something out, the way anyone does.</p>

<p>I'm not writing these for an audience. I'm writing them because I won't remember exactly how I felt on an ordinary Tuesday, and you might want to know one day. Read them in any order. Skip the ones that don't interest you. Some will land differently depending on how old you are when you find them — that's fine, that's kind of the point.</p>
</div>
